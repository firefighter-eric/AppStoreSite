Privacy Policy

App Name: ClipClip

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

ClipClip respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ClipClip handles information.

Overview

ClipClip is a local-first iOS clipboard history app. It helps you save text and links that you choose to keep, search them, edit them, pin them, delete them, and copy them back to the system clipboard.

Clipboard Access

ClipClip does not automatically monitor, read, or save your clipboard in the background, at launch, or when returning to the foreground. Clipboard content is read only after you tap the action to save the current clipboard.

Manual Entries

If you manually add or edit text or links in ClipClip, that content is saved only inside the app's local history. Manual add and edit actions do not require reading the system clipboard.

Local Storage

Saved clipboard records are stored locally on your device. ClipClip does not use remote servers, analytics services, CloudKit, or automatic cloud sync for its core functionality.

Backup Export

ClipClip lets you export your saved records as a JSON backup file through the system file exporter. You choose where that exported file is saved. If you choose a cloud location such as iCloud Drive, that storage is handled by Apple's system Files app and your own device settings.

Data Collection

ClipClip does not collect, transmit, store, or share your personal data on external servers.

Data Sharing

ClipClip does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your Control

You control what content you save into ClipClip. You can edit or delete individual records, clear all saved history from the app, export a backup file, or remove the app from your device.

Children's Privacy

ClipClip does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
