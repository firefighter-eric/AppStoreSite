About ClipClip

ClipClip is an iOS clipboard history app for saving text and links that you choose to keep. It is designed to be local-first and privacy-first.

Support

For any of the following, please email us:

  • Technical issues
  • Bug reports
  • Feature requests
  • Questions about backup export or local data
  • General feedback

Privacy Notes

ClipClip does not automatically monitor your clipboard. Clipboard content is read only when you choose to save the current clipboard into the app.
